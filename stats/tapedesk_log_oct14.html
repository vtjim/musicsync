Fixed
The Mac's Music app has no "Optimize Mac Storage" setting. What works is a smart playlist of tracks whose iCloud Status is "Uploaded", then Remove Download. The Music library on disk went from 126 GB to about 2 GB of local audio, and every track still streams from iCloud.
